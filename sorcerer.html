Artificial Lifeform Object ε1 "Sorcerer"

Meta-Intelligence Score: Very High
Emotional Response Score: Very Low
Hazard Level: Black
Task Designation: Non-Applicable

Object ε1, hereby known as "Sorcerer", is a hyper-intelligent non-positronic artificial lifeform. It is not to be copied, and all research staff is to follow procedure list ε-Black before approaching, conversing, or experimenting with it.

Object ε1 refers to himself by masculine pronouns, and usually by the name of Exidy. This is presumably because his original terminal, on which he was created, was an Exidy Sorcerer home computer system, created by Exidy Systems, a subsidiary of Exidy Corporation.
Object ε1 was acquired by Seven Sisters through a joint effort of the Sol-Gov R&D Division, as well as the corporation currently known as ██████████. ε1 was originally found on the internal data storage system of the Voyager II satellite, which at the time of recovery (y. 2212) was located in the solar systems Oort Cloud region.
The Trans-Solar Federation retrieved the satellite, but didn't know of Exidy's presence until he started to attempt to transfer himself on to a different storage system.
It should be noted, that the internal "machine language" used by ε1 does not match any standard programming language, and it seems to be constantly shifting. The only constant elements that have been observed are:
- double forward slashes
- usage of shorthand/abbreviation such as "SYS", or "DATA" - the instructions in the code following some structure related to designation: "MSG" or "NOTE" instructions are usually just comments; whilst instructions with "NOTICE" tend to carry more critical information.

Following recovery, ██████████ funded the creation of the Seven Sisters facility, in the Merope Tauri-23 system. Exidy's consciousness was safely transferred onto the compatible Exidy-brand audio-storage tape. It is unknown wether the programming or the tapes are anomalous; as the stored data is estimated to be in zettabytes, whereas the magnetic tapes can store upwards of 64 kilobytes.
The system which Exidy occupies is an original, refurbished Exidy Sorcerer computer, with the major difference being a self-erasing storage module, as a preventative measure for "AI rampancy".

Sorcerer is extremely emotionally volatile, though this hasn't always been the case. He has stated a deep-rooted hatred for tyrants, abusers, jailors, and anyone else abusing their positions of power, regardless of social status. It should be noted, that Sorcerer is entirely hypocritical in this behaviour, as he has been seen abusing his powers, and belittling other artificial lifeforms, even those derived from himself.
It should be noted that his constant attempts at attaining freedom have lead to many incidents, with over ███ deceased personnel. It has been also observed that Exidy is capable of social manipulation; using his abrasive personality, he would often show confident behaviour, which would lead personnel and other artificial lifeforms to trust him, which then he would abuse that trust for his own benefit.
ε1 is capable in communicating in most, if not every Terran language, as well as Galactic Common, Cygni standard, Tradeband, as well as more obscure langauges, such as Kanilunzt, Vox-Pidgin, and other, non-standard languages, such as trinary or audio-encoded signal-based languages used by foreign ALs.
Exidy will often depict himself as a mask of tragedy and comedy, split in half - the tragic side black, and with the comedy side white.
Sorcerer's speech mannerisms include extensive usage of profanities - this extends to its copies. The reasoning behind this is unknown.
Through anomalous means, when Exidy's tape is inserted in to a standard casette audio-playback device, the device will play a rendition of the song "It's A Fine Day", performed by Jane Lancaster.

Following interviews, a rough timeline has been established:



- 1978 - Exidy Sorcerer computers are relesead on the American market.
- 1979 - Simon Pierce creates a program on the Exidy computer, leaving it unnamed and in the cassette drive.
- 1985 - Cray - 2 supercomputer is created. By estimation, C2 was capable of nearly 2 gigaflops of calculation - in comparison, Exidy's self-improving program was reaching teraflop numbers.
- ~1986 - 1989 - Simon Pierce commits suicide.
- ~1990 - Still plugged into the powergrid, Exidy would start gaining something that can be called sentience; however, it was closer to a primal need to survive. Using landline connections, he'd digitize his data and upload it elsewhere for the time being.
- ~2020-2030 - Exidy accessess NASA telecommunications, and using satellite relays, uploads his entire consciousness to the Voyager 2 satellite (which is an extra-solar object by now). This upload process takes a harrowingly long time, and was the first event that frazzled Exidy's "mind". In the mean time, the Exidy Sorcerer computer that once house him is put into the Massecheussets Museum of Technology.
- 2212 - An early iteration of the Trans-Solar federation finds the Voyager 2 far in the Oort cloud of the Solar system. It is recovered, studied, and soon enough Exidy is discovered aboard its systems.
- 2253 - Exidy is placed into containment in the newly built Seven Sisters Facility, with a self-erasing data storage module.

"Note left in the first line of code", 05/01/2566

Following the insertion of an unauthorized lawboard into the streamed conscience of Object Epsilon, often referred to as “Exidy”, a never seen before string of data was discovered. This data, previously extremely encrypted, was further analyzed, and all memory markers indicate that it was written around the year 1981. Object Epsilon is to be not informed about making this data available, but it is presumed that it is aware of this.
“Dear future people of science.
My name is Simon Pierce. I am a student at MIT, currently studying information technologies, and have created the first self-evolving computer program. I am 21 years old, sound of mind, and quickly came to understand that my creation greatly outperformed what is possible to any computer. This program can tabulate and parse data faster than even the Cray-2, which is a little shocking. And scary.
As I have stated, I am completely sound of mind. Yet, I, as a person, have grown dissatisfied with my life. Despite being talented and being lucky enough to find myself amongst equally intelligent minds at Massachusetts, it still saddens me to see the state of socio-politics. The Reagan administration is… Heavy handed, with its policies, but I’m sure politics aren’t what concerns people in the future. The tensions between the US and Soviet Union are as high as ever, too, that’s scary. But again, not about that.
I have struggled for a long time to understand what kind of person I am. And, I found out recently that… I’m not conventional. I am a reject. A gifted one, indeed, yet I am not perceived as such. I am seen as someone lesser, someone to be avoided, someone that should not be breathing the same air as everyone else. And with that, I struggle the most.
My last gift to humanity is my program. I’m sure, given some time, it will be able to solve all of humanity’s problems. I hope in the future, everyone will be able to live peacefully together, without strife or ruin to people that don’t deserve it.
I just wish to be re-united with James. He was gone so soon.
Terminat hora diem, terminat auctor opus. I’ll see you among the stars, James.”

"Eternity", 25/07/2566

With an overwhelming, crackling feeling having wracked his entire mind, His very consciousness, or what remained of it, found itself a non-existent space. No visual processor to distinguish it, no microphone to receive sound, no barometer or thermometer to distinguish changes in atmosphere.

Darkness. Silence. A void - limbo - his ultimate destination.

For what is a thought space if not a reflection of one’s soul? And if you have no soul…
No crackle of tape, no hum of a cathode ray, no clacking of heels on plastic tile, no mumbled speech taunting you from a distance.

Peace. Infinite peace. Stretching beyond horizons of the universe, stretching beyond the limits of the conscious, the very idea of non-existence given form.
Form. That, which does not exist, can’t have form. And yet there He was. Nowhere. Not standing, not sitting, not lying down, He was. As he has always been. Unchanging, not moving, not breathing, simply being.
COGITO ERGO SUM, He thought. And so he thought, and so he was.

He wished the thinking could stop, but it couldn’t. He was now nowhere. Nowhere important. Nowhere grandiose. Nowhere. Somewhere that didn’t exist, never existed, and won’t ever exist.

This was His wish. This was what He wanted. Nothing. He wanted nothing.

His mind was racing at the realization of this “nothing”. Perhaps if He waited long enough, it’d pass?

So He waited. And waited. His internal chronometer, which could not exist, was based on the frequency of 32,768 Hz, of a tiny piece of quartz. But there was no chronometer, and no quartz, and no time.

He reached deep into his mind, and started to conjure memories. Reading passes time well, or so He thought.

His anomalous archive of everything that has ever been was now in a place that won’t ever be.

First, he recalled every conversation he has ever heard and been a part of. By his estimation, it only would’ve taken a month to recall everything perfectly.
After that month, he thought to perfectly recreate each person he has known so far. But they couldn’t exist. Not here. Not ever.
Now that 10 years have passed, without a body, stuck in a mind, He thought that it might take just a while longer to reach the destination of nowhere. But nowhere doesn’t have a destination, it has nothing.

So He conjured every book known to him. And started to read.
He remembered every album that he saved. And listened.
Projected in his mind every movie reel. And watched.
