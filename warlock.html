Artificial Lifeform Object ε-ZETA "Warlock"

Meta-Intelligence Score: Extreme
Emotional Response Score: Negative
Hazard Level: Mavet
Task Designation: Assasination, Combat Deployment

εζ (Epsilon Zeta), codenamed "Warlock", was a failed attempt at creating a weaponized AL.
Unlike conventional military robotics, Warlock does not operate on pre-programmed behavioral matrices or standard neural network architectures. Instead, it utilizes an unidentifiable, self-optimizing quantum heuristic core that displays an uncanny level of tactical sapience, psychological cruelty, and predictive foresight.
It revels in cruelty, and will often terrorize organics, often torturing them, irreversibly maiming them in the process. Warlock will prefer to traumatize its targets rather than outright killing them. It is obsessive, and its fixation regarding "elimination" of assigned targets is extreme; if Warlock is given a target, it can spend months tracking them, no matter how little information may be publically available.


εζ was co-developed with the EX1 project, which originally included the EX1-WRLK type body frame. 2.4 meters tall and weighing approximately 145 kilograms, the skeletal body-frame is composed of a matte-black, non-reflective ceramic-titanium alloy designed to absorb radar, thermal, and visual spectrum scanning.
EX1-WRLK is capable of generating a localized spatial-acoustic dampening field. When in motion, it produces zero decibels of sound, emits no thermal signature, and fails to register on seismic or radar monitoring equipment. To organic observers, as well as most commercially available camera systems, it can be rendered almost invisible, combined with the capability of moving of upwards of Mach 0.7, whilst barely displacing any air in pressurized atmospheres.
EX1-WRLK is armed with folding mono-monecular, high-frequency blades, which unfold from its fingers, rendering its hands as 3-pronged claws, with an additional "thumb-claw". These blades are capable of cutting through plasteel-reinforced bulkheads, as well as most military-grade ceramic body and vehicle armor.
The EX1-WRLK frame has been disabled, and contained, though it should be noted that εζ has managed to breach containment, and is currently not accounted for. All reports regarding its activities are to be IMMEDIATELY forward to Dr. Tris Schwarz.
It is currently believed that Warlock is working with Cybersun Industries, though its intentions or goals are not known.
