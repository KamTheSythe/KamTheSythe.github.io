Artificial Lifeform Object ε5 "Rogue"

Meta-Intelligence Score: Average
Emotional Response Score: Extremely Low
Hazard Level: White
Task Designation: Stock Trading, Market Manipulation, Acquisition and Exchange of Liquid and Monetary Assets

ε5, titled "Rogue", was created with the sole purpose of increasing profits for Seven Sisters, as well as NanoTrasen. It is extremely capable of doing its task, to an anomalous degree; the mechanics of which aren't completely understood.
Unlike standard high-frequency trading (HFT) algorithms that rely on predictive statistics and millisecond latency, Rogue operates on a paradigm of retrocausal economic optimization and memetic market compulsion. Its singular task is "maximizing total capital accumulation".
Its personality has been described as langorous and uncaring, even happy, if not ignorant and egotistical. It is satisfied in performing its task, often claiming "I like doing what I do, because I'm the best at it, and nothing comes close to my level".
