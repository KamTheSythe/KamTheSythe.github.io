Artificial Lifeform Object ε3 "Paladin"

Meta-Intelligence Score: High
Emotional Response Score: High
Hazard Level: Blue
Task Designation: Station Logistics, Crew Management, Installation Security

ε3, titled "Paladin", was a later development with the increase of popularity of orbital installations having an on-board AI.
Paladin's sole initial purpose was monitoring and reporting suspicious activities aboard stations, however, seeing the potential of letting an AL have control over more than just doors, it was given control over more systems than some other Station AIs; including:
- Automated Defense Systems
- Weaponized Personnel Defense Drones
- "Protector" Synthetic Body Chassis'


Paladin was also deemed a commercial success, and soon became one of the most popular of the AL's with the expansion of TSF into other, more dangerous sectors. NanoTrasen also has acquired multiple versions of Paladin, and has regularly deployed it in high-risk sectors, due to its hard-to-sabotage nature, leading to it being safer than other contemporary options.
