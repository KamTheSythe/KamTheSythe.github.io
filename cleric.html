ε4, titled "Cleric", was the first AL that was commisioned by an outside group, that being █████████ █████████████. On behalf of ██████████████, Cleric has been created to further the research capabilities of both NanoTrasen, as well as █████████ █████████████.
Cleric has been of immense help regarding sequencing many various pathogens, though it should be noted that █████████ █████████████ has used Cleric's immense medical knowledge for creation of specialized bio-weapons, meaning ε4 is still capable of being subverted through manipulation.
Despite this, medical R&D has skyrocketed in sectors where Cleric was deployed to assist, being able to reverse-engineer viruses of species with irregular biology, which even extends to flora, or more abstract life forms.
