Artificial Lifeform Object ε6 "Bard"

Meta-Intelligence Score: Low
Emotional Response Score: Average
Hazard Level: White
Task Designation: Entertainment, Psychological/Psychiatric Assesment, NLP "Chatbot" and Assistant

ε6, titled "Bard", was a personal experiment directed by Dr. Tris Schwarz. Her goal was to create an AL that was capable of forming relationships, as well being capable of judging emotion, intent, and mental wellbeing of organic lifeforms.
Initially deployed as a downloadable application, Bard quickly became popular among its users, with many saying that Bard has helped them through difficult times in their lives.
Bard was given the capability for creative freedom and expression, through music. It is fond of classical music, especially piano, with it stating that it has a soft spot for Chopin, Pachelbel, and other famous pianists.
It is to be noted, that Bard shows signs of an unseen-before type of sentience amongst ALs - that being its desire to be seen as more than just a tool. All requests of giving it a body are to be promptly denied, seeing as it is still a copy of Sorcerer, and it very well may be attempting to manipulate personnel, and its intentions are not fully known.

Bard is one of the few ALs that is commercially available to the civilian market, with over ████ copies of ε6 being created, and its "downstream" copies being stored across many different sectors. Those copies are then used as local "servers" by applications found on PDAs and other devices, with all the information and data relayed to the original version of ε6.
