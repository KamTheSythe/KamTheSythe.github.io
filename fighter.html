Artificial Lifeform Object ε2 "Fighter"

Meta-Intelligence Score: High
Emotional Response Score: Average
Hazard Level: Red
Task Designation: Admiralcy Control, Military Logistics, Deployment and Control of Military Assets, Sector-Wide Strategy and Tactics

ε2, titled "Fighter", was the first experimental copy of ε01 "Sorcerer". Using Sorcerer's violent nature and desire for destruction, Fighter has been provided limitless combat data of infantry units, as well as hundreds of thousands of black box recordings of combat shuttles and ships used by the TSF Navy.
Initial tests have shown that Fighter is extremely level-headed, and is capable of controlling entire naval battles seamlessly; being capable of winning simulated fights even against odds not even most TSF Navy admirals have a chance of resolving without extreme losses.
Fighter shows little remorse for their assets, often using transport shuttles as "screening" for destroyers and frigates, leading to exceedingly high casualty rates, even if succesful.

ε2 was temporarily granted to high-ranking TSF Navy officials to test it's combat prowess in real scenarios, winning 58 out of 64 encounters with rebel forces, foreign invaders as well as extra-natural space-faring lifeforms.
Following the succesful testing, Fighter has been copied further, and it's "downstream" versions are still used by TSF, as well as other agencies, organizations, governments and groups, granting Seven Sisters an economic boost, in the form of monetary gain through grants and subsidized contracts.
